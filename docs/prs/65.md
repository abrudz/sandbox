# Send a Content Security Policy and other security headers

Closes #65. Part of #1.

## Summary

runner-api sends `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` with every response, and a Content Security Policy that lets the page load scripts, styles, fonts and images and make requests from its own origin only, with no plugins, no `<base>`, no form submissions and no framing (`frame-ancestors 'none'`). The page's four inline `onclick` attributes and its one inline `style` attribute move into `frontend.js` and `frontend.css`, so the policy needs no `'unsafe-inline'`. runner-api serves the page both locally and behind Caddy, so both get the headers, and the browser suite runs under the policy.

## How it was tested

Integration tests read the headers of the page, its files, `/api/v1/version` and `/healthz`, and the policy's directives. A browser test records every policy violation while it closes a message, adds and removes arguments, runs a program and makes a permalink; every other browser test runs under the policy too.

**Decisions in the tests**

- The policy test requires `default-src 'self'`, `object-src 'none'`, `base-uri 'none'`, `form-action 'none'` and `frame-ancestors 'none'`, and refuses `'unsafe-inline'`, `'unsafe-eval'`, `*` and any other origin in every directive.
- The browser test listens for `securitypolicyviolation` events from the first script on each page load, and exercises what the inline attributes did: closing a message, adding and removing arguments. It also runs a program and makes a permalink. It passes without a policy; it is the check that the policy breaks nothing.

- `TestThePageAllowsOnlyItsOwnOriginAndNoFraming`: the page has no policy.
